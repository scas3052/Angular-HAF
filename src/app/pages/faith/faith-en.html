Statement of Faith

Our core beliefs and values

The Bible

We believe the Bible is the inspired, infallible Word of God, serving as our ultimate guide for faith and practice.

All Scripture is breathed out by God and profitable for teaching, for reproof, for correction and for training in righteousness. - 2 Timothy 3:16

Salvation

We believe salvation comes through faith in Jesus Christ and His sacrificial death and resurrection.

For by grace you have been saved through faith. And this is not your own doing; it is the gift of God - Ephesians 2:8

The Holy Spirit

We believe in the presence and power of the Holy Spirit, who guides, comforts, and empowers believers.

But the Helper, the Holy Spirit, whom the Father will send in my name, he will teach you all things and bring to your remembrance all that I have said to you. - John 14:26

The Church

We believe the church is the body of Christ, called to worship, fellowship, and service.

Now you are the body of Christ and individually members of it. - 1 Corinthians 12:27

Service

We believe in serving others as an expression of God's love and our faith in action.

For you were called to freedom, brothers. Only do not use your freedom as an opportunity for the flesh, but through love serve one another. - Galatians 5:13

Love

We believe in demonstrating God's love through compassion, acceptance, and care for all people.

Beloved, let us love one another, for love is from God, and whoever loves has been born of God and knows God. - 1 John 4:7

Prayer

We believe in the power of prayer as a vital connection with God, transforming lives and circumstances.

Do not be anxious about anything, but in everything by prayer and supplication with thanksgiving let your requests be made known to God. - Philippians 4:6

Mission

We believe in spreading the Gospel to all nations, making disciples and sharing God's love worldwide.

Go therefore and make disciples of all nations, baptizing them in the name of the Father and of the Son and of the Holy Spirit. - Matthew 28:19

Sanctification

We believe in the ongoing process of sanctification, where believers are transformed into Christ's likeness through the work of the Holy Spirit.

Now may the God of peace himself sanctify you completely, and may your whole spirit and soul and body be kept blameless at the coming of our Lord Jesus Christ. - 1 Thessalonians 5:23

Repentance

We believe in true repentance as the foundation of our faith. It is a complete turning away from sin and turning to God with a transformed heart and renewed mind. Through genuine repentance, we experience God's forgiveness and the power to live a holy life.

If we confess our sins, he is faithful and just to forgive us our sins and to cleanse us from all unrighteousness. - 1 John 1:9
