Origin of Work

The journey of Holy Army Fellowship

1997

The Beginning

Holy Army Fellowship was founded with a vision to spread God's love and build a community of faithful servants dedicated to worship and service.

2006

Church Established

Our Church was established in Mandavalli, marking the beginning of our physical presence in the community.

2015

Growth and Expansion

The fellowship expanded to multiple districts in Andhra Pradesh and Telangana, reaching more communities with God's message.

Present

Continuing the Mission

Today, Holy Army Fellowship continues to grow and serve, with multiple churches across Andhra Pradesh, Telangana, Chennai and Banglore, touching lives and spreading God's message of love and hope.
